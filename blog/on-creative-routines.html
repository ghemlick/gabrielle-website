On creative routines

July 18, 2026

Inspiration is unreliable. What works better for me is a light routine — not a rigid schedule, but a few repeatable cues that make starting easier.

I block a small window of time, clear one surface (physical or digital), and pick one thing to finish before I context-switch. That's usually enough to build momentum.

Protecting energy

The other half is knowing when to stop. Short sessions beat marathon sprints. When I leave something in a good state, it's much easier to come back to it the next day.
