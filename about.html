About

Engineer, educator, and musician — curious by default.

I'm Gabrielle Hemlick, based in Brooklyn. I studied computer science and music at the University of Pennsylvania, graduating summa cum laude and Phi Beta Kappa, and I've spent my career at the intersection of engineering, teaching, and client-facing work.

Most recently, I was a Platform Engineer in IBM Client Engineering, where I led 20+ customer proof-of-concept engagements — designing conversation flows, writing API specs, and demoing watsonx and IBM Cloud tools weekly for public sector and healthcare clients. Before that, I was a software developer at Code.org, a head teaching assistant for Penn's CIS 120 course, and an undergraduate ML researcher at the Perelman School of Medicine.

Outside of tech, I'm a violinist with roots in Lancaster, Pennsylvania — trained at the Lancaster Center for Classical Studies and active in orchestra and chamber music ever since. I also led the Women at IBM NYC business resource group, producing 20+ events a year for the local community.

I pick up skills quickly, care deeply about the people in the room, and like building things that are both technically sound and genuinely useful. I'm exploring what's next — if that sounds like someone you want on your team, say hello.

Music

Two decades of violin — from All-State to Brooklyn.

High school

  • Pennsylvania All-State Orchestra — 2nd chair second violin (PMEA Festival, Erie, 2017). First violinist in Lancaster Catholic High School history to qualify for All-State.
  • Placed 1st at district, regional, and state auditions out of hundreds of violinists across Central PA — a four-year journey through the PMEA festival program.
  • Two-time instrumental finalist, Lancaster Women's Symphony Association Youth Music Competition (2016, 2017).

College & beyond

  • Penn Symphony Orchestra — Vice President and Principal 2nd Violin while double-majoring in CS and music.
  • Summertrios Chamber Music Workshop — violin intern (2018–2022), playing in faculty-led chamber ensembles; now a board member supporting the workshop community.
  • Active performer in New York with the Brooklyn Symphony Orchestra, the Higher Word Orchestra, and Central Presbyterian Church's annual Handel's Messiah.
  • Seasonal front-of-house roles at the Moab Music Festival, an award-winning chamber music festival in Utah.
